H.E.R

Human–AI Interaction Layer

One human. Many intelligences. One truthful interface.

H.E.R gives independent artificial intelligences bounded roles, preserves their first light, and lenses the completed work into one coherent voice—without silent fallback or surrendering the human relationship to any one provider.

Explore H.E.R Build Week work

H.E.R records the structure of collaboration. She does not claim that structural integrity proves factual truth.

The problem

The human relationship is fragmented across machines.

AI users are asked to manage separate providers, histories, routing systems, memories, and invisible fallback behavior. H.E.R keeps the relationship coherent while making the collaboration structure inspectable.

Integrity spine

Architecture

Phone owns experience.
Server owns execution authority.

H.E.R keeps the human experience simple while separating plan authorship, provider execution, local integrity, persistence, and signal into explicit layers with bounded authority.

Experience layer iPhone interaction, streaming, one H.E.R voice, Trace and Pulse surfaces.

OpenAI Build Week 2026

A private, pre-launch system—meaningfully extended during Build Week.

Prior foundation

H.E.R’s product doctrine, provider-neutral constellation, Core and Relay architecture, iPhone and Watch prototypes, and the Trace, Receipt, Drop, Structural Proof, and Pulse concepts existed privately before Build Week.

Build Week extension

The qualifying extension adds durable Drop v0.2 and derivative Structural Proof v0.2 persistence, exact idempotency and conflict laws, frozen recompilation, quarantine, and fail-closed storage behavior.

  • 2,632 Drop persistence assertions
  • 610 Structural Proof persistence assertions
  • 746 canonical Proof assertions
  • Complete Relay release gate: PASS

First embodied Build Week execution

On July 18, 2026, H.E.R completed the first H.E.R Build Week embodied Synth execution through the iPhone interface. Three independent provider routes completed first light. GPT-5.6 Sol then performed the H.E.R synthesis.

The verified local integrity path contained a Trace, Receipt commitment, Drop commitment, sealed route outputs, and a local Proof manifest v0.2. No silent fallback occurred. Reliability qualification continues.

This is a bounded structural execution claim. It does not establish factual truth, universal provider authenticity, answer correctness, public verification, signature validity, or immutable history.

Structural integrity is not factual truth.
H.E.R claims only what the canonical execution record and its hashes bind.

No provider is treated as an oracle. No commitment proves answer correctness. No integrity record silently becomes a judgment about the human beneath it.
