A study in how
we interact with intelligence.

This public website is an illustrative study of H.E.R's visual, sonic and textual interaction language. HER2·0 remains in development; HER2·I is the intended first qualified public iPhone release.

What you can experience here

The experience presents two complementary views. H.E.R's pane illustrates her Ring, contributing intelligences, commentary and readiness. Chat presents the illustrative question, synthesised answer and its expandable Drop. The Drop becomes available when the answer is ready, independently of recorded narration. These are scripted interactions, not live provider execution or qualified measurements.

No synthesisGain(...) or Hₛ measurement is produced. The recorded voice is an illustrative sample, not an accepted production H.E.R voice.

Your controls

Motion can be paused and follows the browser’s reduced-motion preference. The recorded example plays only after you press Play; Voice is selected on by default and can be muted or unmuted without stopping the recorded sequence. Pressing Escape also stops the sequence and sound. You can explore every sample state directly.

What remains future work

The platform section presents iPhone as in development. Other operating systems are possibilities in the design, without announced releases. No app download is offered by this study.

The organization and this preview

HER2:N·I is H.E.R's organization. her2ni.ai is the institutional and research website. The public experience at her2.ai is an illustrative design study, not qualified native product execution.

The authored exchange runs in the browser. It does not submit prompts, capture a microphone, use analytics or store preferences. This describes the authored page code; hosting and access-control services may process requests independently.

Generational continuity

H.E.R will become richer as intelligence advances while retaining a recognisable identity. Each public generation, implementation build and platform release will be identified explicitly.
