A study in how
we interact with intelligence.

This private website explores H.E.R’s visual, sonic and text identity for her2.ai. HER2·I denotes the first public generation; H.E.R 2.0 remains in development.

What you can experience here

A procedural Ring, an authored interaction sequence, an expandable example Drop and optional sample voice from an uploaded recording. The sample modes help us examine the presentation. They are not a live connection to H.E.R, a microphone or a provider.

The example response is scripted. No synthesisGain(...) or Hₛ measurement is produced. The recorded voice is a private illustrative candidate, not an accepted production H.E.R voice.

Your controls

Motion can be paused and follows the browser’s reduced-motion preference. The recorded example plays only after you press Play; Voice is selected on by default and can be turned off, and stops when disabled or when the page becomes hidden. Pressing Escape also stops the sequence and sound. You can explore every sample state directly.

What remains future work

The platform section presents iPhone as in development. Other operating systems are possibilities in the design, without announced releases. No app download is offered by this study.

The organization and this preview

HER2:N·I is H.E.R's organization. her2ni.ai is the public website. The infrastructure displayed on this website is illustrative only and is separate from the her2.ai deployment.

The authored exchange runs in the browser. It does not submit prompts, capture a microphone, use analytics or store preferences. This describes the authored page code; hosting and access-control services may process requests independently.

Generational continuity

H.E.R will become richer as intelligence advances while retaining a recognisable identity. Each public generation, implementation build and platform release will be identified explicitly.
